## About the Sinhala booklet conversion

The paritha.org PDF does not store Sinhala letters. It stores Latin characters (for example `kfuda ;iai N.jf;da`) that an old font draws as Sinhala. I built a converter that turns these into real Sinhala text, and checked every one of the 142 characters the booklet uses against how the PDF actually draws it.

- **What was checked:** the character table, plus four full pages compared by eye against the printed pages (booklet pages 11, 13, 34 and 37). All four matched line for line.
- **What was not checked:** the other 35 pages, line by line. That is the review this file asks for.
- **Spelling is the booklet's, not mine.** Where the booklet has a misprint, the misprint is kept. Three I noticed while checking:
  - booklet page 11 prints `වෙස්සඥ නායකං` (the name is usually written වෙස්සභූ);
  - booklet pages 10 and 11 print `සිදධත්ථ` without the hal on ද;
  - the foreword prints `උව්ඡාරණය`.
- **One thing was tidied, and only one:** in seven places the booklet has a stray ි typed straight after ේ (`ලැබේ` + ි). On the printed page the two marks overlap and it reads as plain `ලැබේ`, so that is what the file shows.
- **A list of doubtful lines** is at the very end of file 01, under "Lines worth a closer look". Start there.
- **Two-column verse pages** (booklet pages 9–11) are shown row by row as `left | right`. Whether to read down the left column first is still to be confirmed.
- **Verse-beside-meaning pages** (the suttas from booklet page 13 on) print the Pali on the left and the Sinhala meaning on the right. The file shows each block of verse followed by the meaning printed beside it. The pairing follows position on the page and may be a few lines off in places.
- **Reuse:** the booklet says "දහම් පඬුරක් ලෙස, නොමිලේ බෙදාහැරීම පිණිසයි", and paritha.org says its content may be reused for free distribution only. The PDF's own settings have text copying switched off, so a short note to paritha.org before the text ships in the app would be courteous.

## How to check, and what to send back

1. Open the paritha.org PDF beside `01_sinhala_pali_booklet_paritha.md`. Each section of the file is one booklet page.
2. Read a page in both. Note anything that differs from the PDF: a wrong letter, a missing line, lines in the wrong order.
3. Tell me the booklet page number and the line. If the same wrong letter appears more than once, one example is enough; the converter fix then corrects every occurrence.

Where things are stored:

- `source_material/` — the PDFs and the namo.lk download, untouched.
- `content/raw_extract/` — the raw text exactly as it comes out of each PDF, before conversion.
- `content/extracted/` — the structured `.json` versions.
- `tools/` — the scripts that produced all of this, so any step can be re-run.
